05 About TAFKEEK

Design, built on information.

CoverageRemote Worldwide · Based in Egypt
AvailabilityGMT+2
RegistrationSCE-Eligible
LanguagesArabic · English

TAFKEEK is a design studio working at the point where interior and exterior design intent has to survive contact with construction — translating design decisions into coordinated, buildable Revit models at LOD 350 and above, and keeping architecture, structure and MEP disciplines talking to each other before conflicts reach site.

The studio delivers full BIM lifecycles: master planning and facade design, clash-free multidisciplinary coordination, construction-ready shop drawings, automated BOQ extraction and statutory municipal licensing — delivered end-to-end for private and commercial clients across Egypt, Saudi Arabia, the UAE and Qatar, with the same remote-first workflow open to clients anywhere in the world.
