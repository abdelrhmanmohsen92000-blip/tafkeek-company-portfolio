01 Overview

Spaces with architectural character.

TAFKEEK's interior work covers luxury and everyday residential interiors — modern, contemporary and neo-classic — plus workspace and retail interiors for commercial clients, developed with the same material, lighting and spatial-planning discipline as the studio's architectural work.

Where a render is not confirmed to belong to a specific named project, it is captioned generically rather than attributed to one.

02 Approach

From mood to material.

Interior concepts are developed in the same Revit environment used for the rest of the studio's BIM workflow — so furniture layouts, ceiling details and material schedules carry through cleanly into technical documentation rather than being re-drawn separately.

01

Material & Lighting
